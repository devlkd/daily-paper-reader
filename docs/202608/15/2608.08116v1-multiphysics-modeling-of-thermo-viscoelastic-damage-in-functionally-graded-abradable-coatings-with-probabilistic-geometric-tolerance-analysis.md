## 摘要
在航空发动机中，功能梯度可磨耗涂层用于控制叶尖间隙，但其耐久性受多种效应支配，而这些效应在现有模型中常被分别处理，包括温度相关的粘弹性软化、渐进损伤、沉积引起的微观结构调制和几何公差。本研究将这些效应整合在一个统一的多物理场-概率框架中。求解域为局部沿厚度方向的涂层柱，由规定的应变和温度历史驱动，其中包含热本征应变和涂层-基底膨胀失配。结果表明，与单调梯度相比，周期性性能调制增加了循环结束时的损伤，并将最大应力集中在局部调制波峰内。通过耦合求解器传播对称几何公差，对于调制梯度产生了强右偏的损伤分布，并比单调梯度具有更高的分级超越概率。这种超越行为无法通过在名义几何条件下进行的确定性分析获得。该实现通过空间和时间细化、闭合形式单元测试、带Wilson置信区间的嵌套蒙特卡洛收敛性以及经留出验证的多项式响应面进行了验证。数值验证与实验验证被明确区分，且模拟案例作为通用基准而非经辨识的材料模型呈现。该框架将沉积参数和公差带与损伤超越风险联系起来，支持面向可靠性的梯度及多层涂层系统筛选。

## Abstract
In aircraft engines, functionally graded abradable coatings are used to control blade-tip clearance, but their durability is governed by effects that are often treated separately in existing models, including temperature-dependent viscoelastic softening, progressive damage, deposition-induced microstructural modulation, and geometric tolerances. This study integrates these effects within a unified multiphysics--probabilistic framework. The solved domain is a local through-thickness coating column driven by prescribed strain and temperature histories that include thermal eigenstrain and coating--substrate expansion mismatch. The results show that periodic property modulation increases end-of-cycle damage relative to the monotonic gradient and concentrates the maximum stress within a localized modulation crest. Propagating symmetric geometric tolerances through the coupled solver produces a strongly right-skewed damage distribution and a higher classification-level exceedance probability for the modulated gradient than for the monotonic gradient. This exceedance behavior cannot be obtained from a deterministic analysis performed at nominal geometry. The implementation is verified through spatial and temporal refinement, closed-form unit tests, nested Monte Carlo convergence with Wilson confidence intervals, and a hold-out-validated polynomial response surface. Numerical verification is clearly distinguished from experimental validation, and the simulated case is presented as a generic benchmark rather than an identified material model. The framework links deposition parameters and tolerance bands to damage-exceedance risk, supporting reliability-oriented screening of graded and multilayer coating systems.
