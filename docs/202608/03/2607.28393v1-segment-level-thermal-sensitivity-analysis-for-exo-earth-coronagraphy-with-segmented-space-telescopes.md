## 摘要
类地行星的直接成像和表征需要超稳定的波前，以在宿主星的星冕仪暗区实现100万亿分之一（ppt）的星光抑制水平。望远镜的热漂移可能影响波前稳定性。在此背景下，我们提出了一个对类地行星探测至关重要的分段空间望远镜分段级热稳定性误差预算，并明确了超稳定望远镜的要求。我们的研究使用了多种分段主镜架构，每种架构都配有空间望远镜科学研究所分段星冕仪设计与分析研究团队为离轴约6米孔径空间望远镜设计开发的相应切趾器方案。利用L3Harris Technologies提供的详细有限元模型，我们将主镜位置处的温度梯度与每个分段上的波前变化联系起来。我们分别采用基于配对的空间分段望远镜成像分析模型（PASTIS）灵敏度方法和批量估计算法，为每个分段分配了静态和动态热容差。分析表明，由于切趾作用，主镜所有分段的容差分配不均匀，并且随着分段数目的增加，外侧分段的容差要求通常变得不那么严格。我们观察到，为了达到所需的暗穴对比度，分段尺寸、容差放宽与最佳波前传感时间之间存在权衡。

## Abstract
Direct imaging and characterization of Earth-like planets require ultra-stable wavefronts to achieve a starlight suppression level of 100 parts per trillion (ppt) in the coronagraphic dark region of the host star. Thermal drifts in the telescope may affect the wavefront stability. In this context, we present a segment-level thermal stability error budget for segmented space telescopes crucial to Earth-like planet detection and specify requirements for an ultra-stable telescope. Our study utilizes multiple segmented primary mirror architectures, each with their respective apodizer solution developed by the Segmented Coronagraph Design & Analysis research team at the Space Telescope Science Institute, tailored to an off-axis ~6 m-aperture space telescope design. Using a detailed finite element model provided by L3Harris Technologies, we relate the temperature gradient at the location of the primary mirror to wavefront variations on each segment. We allocate both static and dynamic thermal tolerances for each segment using the Pair-based Analytical model for Segmented Telescope Imaging from Space sensitivity approach, and a batch-estimation algorithm respectively. Our analysis shows a non-uniform tolerance allocation across all segments of the primary mirror, as a result of apodization, and generally, with an increase in segment numbers, the tolerances for outer segments become less stringent. We observe trade-offs between segment size, tolerance relaxation and optimal wavefront sensing time to achieve a desired dark-hole contrast.
